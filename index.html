drafts

every draft is checkedbefore you see it

Each draft draws on your saved replies, your instructions, the customer's orders and the thread. Every quote and link in it is matched against those sources.

  • Quote found in Moving a license
  • Link to yourstore.com/docs/license is in your sources
  • Link to yourstore.com/pricing isn't, so the draft is marked unverified

agents

bring your own agent,it can answer too

Bridle opens your inbox to agents like Claude, ChatGPT and OpenClaw through WordPress abilities over MCP. Connect one with Saddle or any MCP client, and it can read a conversation, check your saved replies and leave a draft for you.

Sending needs a permission you grant and an inbox in Auto mode, with the same guardrails as Bridle's own agent.
